About us

Three generations of Stringfellow

MJ Stringfellow & Sons was established in 1969 and is now run by the third generation of the family. For over five decades we've been providing construction and maintenance services from our base in Coppull, near Chorley.

We work for private homeowners, local authorities, commercial architects and housing associations, with time-served tradesmen and trusted subcontractors. We can draw up plans, help with planning applications and deal with Building Control, so you have one team from the first idea to the finished job.
